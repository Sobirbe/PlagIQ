Оплата, возвраты и проверка сертификата

Если проверка не выполнилась по технической причине, зарезервированное для неё возвращается автоматически: бесплатная проверка, тестовый лимит или повторная проверка — на ваш аккаунт, оплаченная сумма — на баланс PlagIQ (если не сработал ИИ-детектор или сравнить было нечего — соответствующая часть). Так же и при перезапуске сервера, прервавшем проверку. Запросы о возврате денег на карту: {{EMAIL}}. {{COUNSEL}}

QR-код и ссылка в сертификате содержат подписанные данные проверки (номер отчёта, дата, процент, число слов, начало отпечатка SHA-256 документа). Для их подтверждения на сервере не хранятся ни документ, ни отчёт, поэтому сертификат проверяется и после удаления отчёта. Автор, название и тип документа, указанные для сертификата, хранятся только вместе с отчётом __KEEPACC_RU__ и в ссылку не входят.

What is sent to others

  • OpenAlex (academic search): up to 3 short excerpts (14 words each) and the DOIs in your reference list, with your consent.
  • Yandex / Brave (web search, separate consent): up to 20 short excerpts (14 words each) from across the document.
  • Pangram (AI detector, USA, separate consent, Russian and English texts only): the full text of the document.
  • Payme / Click / Uzum Bank (if you pay): the order number and amount. Card details are entered on the provider's own page.
  • Telegram: when you sign in, Telegram gives us your ID, name and username. If you signed in with Telegram and the page was closed when your check ended, the bot sends you a message with the file name and a link to “My checks” (no percentage, no document text).
  • Resend (e-mail delivery, if you sign in by e-mail): your address and the message with the one-time sign-in code or link; if the page was closed when your check ended, a “your check is ready” message with the file name and a link to “My checks” (no percentage, no document text).
  • Railway (hosting where PlagIQ’s servers run): all data in the table above is stored on these servers.

These services follow their own privacy policies; deleting a PlagIQ report does not delete what they keep.

Payments, refunds and certificate verification

If a check does not complete for a technical reason, what it reserved is given back automatically: a free check, test allowance or recheck to your account, and the amount paid to your PlagIQ balance (in part, if the AI detector did not run or nothing could be compared). The same happens when a server restart cuts a check off. Requests to refund money to a card: {{EMAIL}}. {{COUNSEL}}

The certificate’s QR code and link carry the check’s signed figures (report number, date, percentage, word count, start of the document’s SHA-256 fingerprint). Confirming them needs neither the document nor the report on the server, so a certificate can still be verified after the report is deleted. The author, title and document type you enter for the certificate are kept only with the report for __KEEPACC_EN__ and are not in the link.
