## Known limits of the rules

- A reference placed more than 80 characters after the closing quote, or only in a footnote the extractor dropped, is reported as *quotation without a nearby reference*.
- Unbalanced or decorative quotation marks can mark the wrong span; quotations over 3,000 characters are ignored.
- A bibliography without a recognised heading is not detected, and text after it is treated as bibliography until an appendix heading.
- Indented block quotations without quotation marks are not recognised.
